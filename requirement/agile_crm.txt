One Important Correction to Our Earlier Design
Previously, I proposed a separate Honda CRM Lead table. After reviewing this process
manual, a better learning design is:
1 Contact = prospect/lead identity
2 Opportunity = potential vehicle sale
3 Activity/Interaction = follow-up work
4 Customer = converted commercial entity
This is closer to the existing implementation and teaches you how to extend Business
Central rather than rebuild its CRM model unnecessarily.
A custom Lead table is justified only if the company needs a separate unqualified inbox
before creating a standard Contact—for example, raw website leads that must be
deduplicated, cleaned, assigned, and accepted first.
A more advanced future design could be:
1 Raw Lead Inbox
2 → Validate and Deduplicate
3 → Accept
4 → Create/Link Standard Contact
5 → Create Opportunity
But that should not be Part 1.
