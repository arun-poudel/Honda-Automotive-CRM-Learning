Why We Are Not Creating a Custom Lead Table
For this project:

•
•
•
•

A Contact represents the prospect or customer identity.
An Opportunity represents one possible vehicle sale.
An Activity represents work or communication.
A vehicle-interest line represents what the customer may purchase.

A single Contact may have several Opportunities over time. For example, the same person
may enquire about a scooter today and an automobile two years later.

Duplicate Rules
A Contact is a possible duplicate when another Contact has:

•
•

The same nonblank mobile/phone number, or
The same nonblank email address

The current Contact must be excluded.

Required Logic
1.
2.
3.
4.
5.
6.
7.
8.

Copy the Contact record into a separate record variable.
Reset filters.
Exclude the current Contact No.
Search first by Phone No.
Search separately by E-Mail.
Avoid showing the same duplicate twice.
Return whether at least one possible duplicate exists.
If ShowResults is true, open a filtered Contact List.

Important Behavior
Duplicate detection is a warning, not a hard error, in Part 1.
Reasons include:
•
•
•

Family members may share a phone number.
Company contacts may use a shared address.
A valid duplicate may represent a separate person or business relationship.

The user must still review existing records before continuing.

Concept Check
Why can’t Get be used to find duplicate phone numbers?
Expected answer:
•
•
•
•

Get reads a record using its primary key.
Phone No. is not the Contact primary key.
There may be multiple Contacts with the same phone number.
SetRange, SetFilter, FindFirst or FindSet are required.

What You Should Understand After Part 1
By the end of this assignment, you should be able to explain this clearly:
A Contact identifies the person or organization with whom the company has a
relationship. An Opportunity represents one potential sale involving that Contact. Vehicleinterest lines describe the products being considered. Activities represent calls, meetings,
visits and follow-ups. The sales cycle and CRM stage control how the Opportunity
progresses. Status-history entries preserve the significant changes that occurred over
time.
